About Us

Motoworx Winton opened its doors in November 2017 as Winton Motorcycles Kawasaki.

Motoworx Winton was formed in 2018 and with that formation Mark and Ruth Howden and Justin and Clare Shaw were welcomed into ownership of Motoworx together with Blair and Alena Howden.

For the past eight years the Shaws and the Howdens have been busy establishing Motoworx as a strong and trusted motorcycle business in Central Southland.

Located at 63 Great North Road, Winton we are at the very heart of Central Southland and also support customers in northern, western, eastern and southern Southland and beyond.

We stock two brands of bikes — Kawasaki and CF Moto and are proud representatives of both of these franchises.

We also offer trade ins and carry a huge variety of accessories and consumables for both bikes and their riders.

We boast a strong, experienced and widely trusted workshop offering services and repairs on all makes and models.

We also offer a range of finance options. Check out our menu for a useful calculator to enable you to crunch the numbers before you buy. We also have handy links to UDC Finance to enable you to build the full picture on your next investment. Also check out our social media for various finance and special offerings provided by Kawasaki and CF Moto seasonally throughout the year. Find the right time and deal for you!
